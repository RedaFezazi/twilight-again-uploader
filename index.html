What it does

The owner starts a batch from their computer. The uploader checks the connected channel, compares existing uploads and schedules, then uploads selected videos with their titles, descriptions and publication times.

A local queue records completed uploads so interrupted batches can resume without blindly uploading the same files again.

How access works

Google handles account sign-in and consent. The desktop uploader requests permission to upload videos and read YouTube account information for channel checks, upload status and scheduling checks. Changing existing release times requires an additional video-management permission, requested separately when the owner needs it. The privacy policy explains that permission's scope.

This website provides app information. It does not accept sign-ins, receive videos or store Google authorization credentials.

This is a personal tool for the channel owner, rather than a public upload service. Read the privacy policy for data handling and how to revoke access.
